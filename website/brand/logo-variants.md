## Recommendation

Proceed with `website/assets/brand/merge-rail/executespec-merge-rail-mark.svg`.

Reason: Merge Rail is the strongest product story. It forms an E from delivery rails and ends in a verified merge, which is closer to ExecuteSpec's core promise than a generic gear or plain monogram. The old gear should be retired from primary usage because it reads as generic automation and its glossy style conflicts with the current dark product theme.
