## 2. The latency finding (why all this matters)

Measured on the mimo OpenRouter run (348 calls): **latency scales with OUTPUT tokens, not input**
(input is 90% cache-hit). ~6s base (TTFT + queuing) + **~30 tok/s** generation. p50 7s, p90 48s, max
107s. 37% of calls >10s.

**Root cause:** OpenRouter serves `xiaomi/mimo-v2.5-pro` via 2 providers — **Xiaomi ($0.435/M, cheap,
slow)** and **DeepInfra ($1.00/M)** — and our client sent **no provider preference**, so OpenRouter
defaulted to *price* routing → the slow Xiaomi backend. It's the provider's serving speed, **not our
code**.

**Fix direction (agreed):**
- **Chinese OPEN models → US fast-inference (Fireworks / Together / DeepInfra-direct), stay us-east.**
  This fixes the generation throughput (the dominant term).
- mimo specifically has no fast native route; try DeepInfra-direct or accept OpenRouter.
- **Zone change (India/Singapore) won't help** — network RTT is ~3% of the latency. If ever splitting
  by region, **Singapore (`asia-southeast1`) beats India** (better to both China + US).
- Free eval-side wins: cap max output tokens, lower Harbor concurrency.
- (Not done) OpenRouter `provider:{sort:"throughput"}` would route mimo to the faster provider —
  marginal + pricier; we pivoted to Fireworks instead.
