### RL-2026-06-026 - Verified score trust caps and evidence ledger
- **Change:** Keep the additive `verifiedScore` formula, then cap the headline when verification signals show review/build/test risk; expose `verificationEvidence[]` on validation reports; extend the SUPER_ADMIN backfill endpoint with `{runId, limit, dryRun}` and recompute both `verifiedScore` and `rubricScore`.
- **Why:** A high internal score without visible evidence reads as self-scored proof. The score still needs to summarize routing quality, but the product surface must show which claims are deterministic, which are LLM assessments, and which are only the internal formula.
- **Tradeoff:** Strong partial work can no longer headline above the cap even if the weighted formula would be higher. This is intentional: unresolved review, build, or test signals should force operator/customer review before external use.
