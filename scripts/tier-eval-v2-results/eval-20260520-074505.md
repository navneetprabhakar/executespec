## Headline findings

1. **Anthropic `claude-sonnet-4-6` did not call `submitOutline` in ANY of the 3 PLANNING cases**
   (0/100, `WRONG_TOOL` × 3). It wrote the plan as prose/JSON text instead (1,933–4,557 output
   tokens, 29–66s). OpenAI `gpt-5.4` and Gemini `gemini-3-pro-preview` both called the tool cleanly
   in all 3. There is **no `tool_choice` forcing** in `LlmRouter`/`OutlinePlannerAgent`, so in
   production this Anthropic behavior falls through to `PlannerOutputUtils.salvagePlanFromText` —
   the fragile text-recovery path, not clean tool-calling. **This is the single most important
   result: the default provider's planner is running on salvage, not a structured tool call.** The
   old text-completion eval could never have caught this — it would have scored Anthropic's text
   output as a pass.

2. **OpenAI is the all-round winner this round** — perfect on classification (100), reliable
   tool-calling on planning (90), fastest (10.5s avg), and **cheapest by 2–4.5×** ($0.082 total vs
   $0.184 Anthropic / $0.373 Gemini). It hit prompt cache on 2 of 6 calls (cache_read 1664–2688).

3. **Gemini `gemini-3-pro-preview` is correct but slow + expensive on PLANNING** — 40–43s/call and
   $0.115–$0.128/call (it emits 4,900–5,500 output tokens per plan, ~4× OpenAI's). Correct, but you
   pay for the verbosity.

4. **Classification (LIGHTWEIGHT) is solid across all three.** The only misses were Anthropic-haiku
   and Gemini-flash both calling the medium SaaS spec `SMALL` instead of `MEDIUM` (one-band miss,
   scored 70). OpenAI-mini nailed all three bands.

5. **Plan task-count quality is weak on the bigger specs (all providers).** On the medium-SaaS and
   JS→TS-migration specs, both OpenAI and Gemini produced only **4 tasks** when the spec implied
   8–14 and 6–12 respectively (scored 85, "out of band"). The planners under-decompose multi-feature
   work — a planning-quality gap independent of provider.
